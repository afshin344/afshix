Product Designer Iran ▼
Rayan Kavesh Hirkan
  • Designed a multi-platform system for a municipal waste collection and recycling program: a PWA for End users, a native Android app for field staff and drivers, and a desktop application for warehouse operations.
  • Built the citizen-facing app as a PWA so it would run on any phone, iPhone or Android, without an install step or update requirement; citizens had no time-sensitive needs, so instant browser access mattered more than deep device integration.
  • Required a native Android app for drivers and field staff instead, because the job depended on background access to GPS and push notifications even when the app wasn't actively open, something a PWA couldn't reliably provide.
  • Chose desktop over mobile for the warehouse application: staff worked at fixed stations and needed to process higher volumes of data per session than a phone screen could comfortably support.
  • Represented the client in coordination and presentation meetings with municipal authorities and the waste management organization, aligning design decisions with regulatory and operational requirements.

    • Staff, driver, and warehouse screens are not shown here due to client confidentiality.
